## Current behaviour

New trips use workflow 2; earlier workflow-1 trips keep their existing routes and immutable history. A six-question guided conversation collects personal availability inside a tentative date option, travel buffers, whole-trip ceiling and personal travel reserve, interests/places, private must-haves and flexible answers. This guide is structured UI, not an LLM call for each question. Nothing is saved until personal-summary confirmation.

The review now includes editable planning requests, each with a type and hard/soft priority. Suggestions are generated locally; the author must check completeness, wording, type and priority and explicitly approve what may be shared. Raw answers remain private. Surfing and scuba diving from the reported positive list appear as separate hard activity requests. Unrecognised or conditional text stays intact for owner editing; the suggestions are not a general language parser. Long/many requests are rejected for editing, never silently truncated or dropped.

Generation waits for every included person's confirmed input, the minimum roster and at least one common hour after personal outbound/return travel buffers. Gemini Free Tier additionally requires TEST · AI labelling, fictional assertions and separate Allow Gemini consent from everyone. Only owner-reviewed shareable requests/interests/places, destination and common times go to Google. Private cities, ceilings, exact personal times, raw private hard/soft answer text, IDs and credentials are excluded. Reviewed request text is intentionally shared; owners must remove private reasons before approving. Existing consent is not silently expanded: summaries saved before this correction must be reopened and freshly reviewed before a new generation.

Gemini orders the approved requests. The server prioritises hard activities, builds a continuous itinerary with meals/rest/stays, includes personal travel reserves within ceilings and records an outcome for every request. Activity requests are scheduled once; requests that cannot fit are explicitly excluded for budget or timing. Meal/stay requests are attached to relevant steps and marked for booking verification. Other unsupported conditions are excluded from the promised plan as unverified; they are never declared satisfied. A single traveller does not get a group poll. The fictional reference allowances are food INR350/block, stay INR1500/night, surfing INR2500, scuba INR4500, sailing INR2000, other activities/local travel INR550, INR50 fees and 10% contingency. These are test assumptions, not real fares, quotes, instructor safety or destination availability checks.

Group alternatives use most votes after all ballots or the deadline; ties remain unresolved. Concerns need a strict majority of the included roster to change a step. Changed activities recalculate cost and requirement outcomes, including exclusions caused by the group decision, and create a new immutable version with fresh reviews. A step change that exceeds a confirmed budget is rejected. Ballots and private reasons remain private. Every included person reviews the exact current version and explicitly acknowledges their own exclusions. An organiser can lock only after all included reviews. Hard concerns require the affected author's acceptance of the current outcome; a majority cannot manufacture that acceptance. Personal availability and ceilings remain limits. Base meals/stays that exceed a ceiling still block agreement. Locks/history remain immutable; locked plans require reopened input before fresh generation.
