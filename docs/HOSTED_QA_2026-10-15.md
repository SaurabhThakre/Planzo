## Fixes

- Signup and sign-in use an account-specific two-column layout at desktop and smaller desktop-preview widths. Illustration is on the left and the form on the right, as the user requested. General trip screens retain their own responsive layouts.
- The account panel uses the original local Goa illustration from the Figma asset exports. Form spacing, name/email layout, buttons, and supporting messages were aligned with the design's mint, forest-green and cream system.
- On phones, the account form comes first, inputs stack, buttons fit the width, and header content fits the narrow screen.
- Whitespace-only organiser names are rejected with a visible message.
- Draft-generation copy says “Every voice. One shared starting point.” so it also fits groups that do not contain six people.

Application changes are limited to `src/entry.tsx`, `src/index.css`, and the single copy string in `src/planning.tsx`. Domain and persistence rules were preserved.

## Limits and next step

The complete-price fixture supports Goa from Bengaluru, three days/two nights, four to six included travellers. Other saved trips can remain blocked when sample catalogue data is missing. Accounts are local identifiers; demo personas and frontend privacy views are not production access controls. Each browser/origin has separate data. Preview and eventual published origins may also have separate demo data.

Authentication, returning-guest verification, cross-device collaboration, Supabase data access, live AI, real speech input, live prices, actual reservations and payments are not connected or validated. A human usability walkthrough and broader browser/device/accessibility checks remain pending.

Next: the team reviews this corrected demo. If it chooses to publish the demo, label it clearly, then verify the published origin separately. Real-user collaboration requires a separately scoped backend implementation and tests.
